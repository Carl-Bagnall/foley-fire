Privacy Notice

How Foley Fire Risk Assessment Ltd collects, uses and protects personal information.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This notice explains what personal information Foley Fire Risk Assessment Ltd collects when we quote for and carry out fire risk assessments and fire door surveys, why we use it, and your rights. It applies to our clients and their staff, the people we meet or hear about at the premises we assess, and anyone who asks us for a quote.

Who we are

Foley Fire Risk Assessment Ltd is the controller of the personal information described here. We decide how and why it is used.

Company Foley Fire Risk Assessment Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 15194339
Registered office 1st Floor Offices, 105 Church Street, Tewkesbury, England, GL20 5AB
ICO registration ZB843328
Contact for data questions info@foleyfire.co.uk

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as the law doesn't require one for a business of our size and type. Questions about your information go to info@foleyfire.co.uk.

Whose information we hold

  • Clients and their contacts: the people we deal with at the businesses, landlords and organisations we work for.
  • People at the premises: responsible persons, managers and staff we speak to, and residents, employees or visitors mentioned in our reports.
  • People who ask for a quote: including those who don't go on to become clients.
  • Contractors and suppliers we work with, such as electricians or door installers named in a report.

What information we collect

TypeExamples
Contact details Name, job title, organisation, address, phone, mobile, email
Details in our reports Who the responsible person is, who we consulted, what we saw and were told about fire safety at the premises
Photographs Pictures of the premises, which can sometimes show people, vehicles or names on documents
Documents you give us Certificates, floor plans and service records, which may include engineers' or managers' names
Quotes and billing What we quoted, prices, and whether work was accepted and invoiced

Occupants at special risk. A fire risk assessment has to consider people who may need help to escape, for example because of a disability or mobility, hearing or sight needs. We describe those needs without naming anyone wherever we can (for example "one wheelchair user on the ground floor"). If a named person's health information is ever recorded, we treat it as special category data and protect it accordingly.

Where we get it from

  • From you, when you contact us, ask for a quote or book work.
  • From our client, when they give us details of their premises, staff or contractors.
  • From the people we speak to and what we see and photograph during our visits.
  • From documents the client provides, such as previous assessments, certificates and plans.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

The law only lets us use personal information when we have a lawful basis. Ours are:

What we use it forLawful basis
Carrying out and reporting on fire risk assessments and fire door surveys Contract: it's needed to do the work our client has engaged us for. For people named in a report who aren't our client: legitimate interests, in producing an accurate fire safety assessment that keeps people safe
Quoting for work and following up a quote Legitimate interests: responding to an enquiry and offering our services. For a sole trader or individual, steps before a contract
Keeping records of the work we did Legitimate interests: showing what we found and advised, and defending any claim about our work
Invoicing and accounts Legal obligation: keeping financial records for HMRC and company law
Answering questions and requests about your data Legal obligation under UK GDPR

Health information. We avoid recording health or disability information about named people. When we plan for people who may need help to escape a fire, we describe their needs without naming them, for example "one wheelchair user on the ground floor". If a named person's health information is ever recorded, we treat it as special category data under UK GDPR: we keep it only for their safety, limit who can see it and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed our interests against yours and keep the information to what's needed. You can object (see Your rights).

We never sell personal information or use it for marketing. We share it only with:

Our service providers act on our instructions under written agreements and may not use the information for their own purposes.

This website (foleyfire.co.uk)

The sections above cover how we handle personal information across our work, including the assessment app we use to prepare reports. This section is about our public website specifically.

  • Contact form: when you send an enquiry through our quote form, your details are delivered to our inbox by Resend, an email delivery service. Resend processes the submission only to pass it to us.
  • Cookies: we don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The anti-spam check on our contact form (Cloudflare Turnstile) may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the form from abuse. It's needed for the form to work and doesn't track you across other websites.
  • Visitor analytics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they use. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't fingerprint you and doesn't track you across other sites; the figures are aggregate only.
  • Fonts: our pages use Google Fonts to display text, and Google may process your IP address to serve them.

Resend and Google are based in the USA and process only the limited information described above, under appropriate data protection safeguards.

Transfers outside the UK

Cloudflare may store or process information outside the UK, including in the EU and the USA. Those transfers are covered by Cloudflare's data processing agreement, part of our subscription agreement with them, which uses the UK's approved terms for international data transfers. Microsoft 365 and OneDrive may also process information outside the UK. Microsoft covers this in its Data Protection Addendum, which also uses the UK's approved transfer terms.

How long we keep it

InformationHow long
Reports, photographs and the records behind them 7 years after our last work for the client, because a claim about our work can be made for up to 6 years
Quotes that weren't taken up 12 months from the quote date
Invoices and accounts 6 years from the end of the financial year, as the law requires
Anything we delete Removed from our systems within 30 days, and from our backups 30 days after that

We review what we hold regularly and delete it when these periods end, unless the law or an open claim means we must keep it longer.

How we keep it secure

  • Our assessment app can only be used by our own staff, who must sign in on each device they use.
  • Information is encrypted when it travels over the internet and when it is stored by Cloudflare.
  • Staff devices that hold information for working offline are protected by a passcode, and their copy is wiped when they sign out.
  • Photos taken in the app have their location data removed.
  • Only staff who need the information for their work can see it, and they are bound by confidentiality.

If something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we will tell the ICO within 72 hours where the law requires it, and tell you if there is a high risk to you.

Automated decisions

We don't make decisions about people by automated means, and we don't use personal information for profiling. People make every decision in our work.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to correct anything that's wrong or incomplete.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your information. We may need to keep some of it, for example a report we're legally or contractually bound to keep, and will explain if so.
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use it while a concern is looked into.
  • Objection: object to us using it on the basis of legitimate interests.
  • Portability: where we use it under a contract, ask for it in a form you can take elsewhere.

To use any of these rights, email info@foleyfire.co.uk. We'll reply within one month, and it's free. We may ask you to confirm who you are first.

Changes to this notice

We'll update this notice when how we use information changes. The latest version is always on our website. Last updated: 12 November 2026.
