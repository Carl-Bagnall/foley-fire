Privacy Policy

How Foley Fire Risk Assessment Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Foley Fire Risk Assessment Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in England and Wales (Company No. 15194339). We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. If you have any questions, you can reach us at info@foleyfire.co.uk or on 01242 379069.

What information we collect

When you get in touch through our quote form or by email, phone or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • your name
  • your email address and phone number
  • details about your property, such as premises type and location
  • any other information you choose to include in your message

If you go on to engage our services, we may also collect the information needed to carry out and deliver a fire risk assessment or related work.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, deliver the services you request and keep records of the work we carry out. Our lawful bases for processing are our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business, and, where you become a client, the performance of a contract with you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the website and communicate with you, including:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the website and provides the anti-spam check on our form
  • Resend, which delivers quote-form submissions to our inbox by email
  • Fasthosts, which provides our email service
  • Google Fonts, which serves the fonts used on the site and may process your IP address to do so

We may also disclose information where we are required to do so by law.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The anti-spam check on our contact form (Cloudflare Turnstile) may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the form from abuse. It is required for the form to work and does not track you across other websites.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as we need it to deal with your request and for a reasonable period afterwards. Where you become a client, we keep records for as long as necessary to provide our services and to meet our legal, insurance and accounting obligations, after which the information is securely deleted.

How we protect your information

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to keep your information secure and to guard against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

International transfers

Some of our service providers may process information on servers outside the United Kingdom. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards so that your information continues to be protected to UK standards.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it and to request a copy in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@foleyfire.co.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised date.
